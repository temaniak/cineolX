# Listen visual specification

Selected concept: `concept.png`, generated with the built-in Image Gen tool.
Native concept dimensions: 1536 × 1024. It is a design reference, not a website asset.

- Charcoal canvas `#101318`; rail `#171c23`; off-white `#f3f5f8`; secondary `#b6bec9`.
- Fine separators `#39424d`; periwinkle accent `#7c9aff`; optional Clean sage `#91bc94`.
- System sans typography: wordmark 34 px; page title 40 px; section titles 22 px;
  body and chrome 18–20 px, captions 14 px. Phone title 30 px.
- Header 70 px, open layout with a 344 px sidebar, main horizontal padding 32 px.
- Sidebar: collection selector, count, search and flat selected/list rows.
- Main: collection breadcrumb, selected-case title/subtitle, equal-width variants,
  real waveform with playhead/ticks, transport, a short switching hint, notes,
  four-column settings and download links. No decorative art or card grid.
- Icons: outlined lock/search/volume/repeat/download/restart, 1.8 px strokes;
  play/pause are solid simple transport symbols, 56 px circular primary button.
- Mobile continuation: sidebar becomes a collapsible selection panel; stacked
  variants remain reachable, waveform/transport stay full width, settings use 2 columns.
- Source text and data remain JSON-driven. Collection title, case names, comments,
  settings and available variants can vary. Additional login/empty/error/buffering
  states are functional requirements; their copy is intentionally outside the main concept.

Image Gen brief: full private audio-comparison studio screen, Russian UI, exact
Listen / Concert Hall V1 / Lexicon IR / Cineol / Cineol Clean labels; waveform,
seek and volume, loop/restart, explanatory comments and settings; restrained dark
flat studio visual language; no decorative cards, art or unrelated analytics.

The implementation may show the actual data waveform rather than the concept's
illustrative waveform. Real selected playback position, duration, notes and
settings take precedence over the example values in the concept.
